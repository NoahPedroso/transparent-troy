Transparent Troy

Find your city council member

About: This is a quick tool meant to help citizens of Troy, NY find the person who represents them in their city council. I'm making this because I thought the tools already available online were not up-to-par with what many people come to expect their digital experience to be like. It is modeled after the "find my congressperson" and "find my assemblymember" sites provided by the US congress and the New York State Assembly.

Sources: My data comes from the rensselaer county website's public shape files: https://www.rensco.com/589/District-Maps (actual download). It has information for every election district in rensselaer county, which of course includes Troy. This project basically just takes that data and adds an interactive wrapper around it, some information, and makes it troy focused. Troy election districts static map: https://troyny.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1731/Election-District-Map-2023-PDF and https://troyny.gov/315/City-Council

NOTES: I could not find the city council district shapefiles themselves, so I am just relying on the eleciton distrit data and static maps of the city council districts, as the election districts seem to be fully contained within city council districts. I hope to find a source telling me that will always be the case. See the SOURCE section to see the static maps.
